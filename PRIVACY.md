# Privacy Policy for Quill

**Effective date: August 20, 2026**

Quill is designed to keep your information under your control.

## Data Not Collected

The Mac App Store version of Quill does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, developer account system, license service, or external updater.

## Snippets and preferences

Your snippets, formatting, embedded images, app-specific rules, preferences, and usage counts are processed on your Mac. By default, they remain in Quill's local app container. Quill does not send this content to Beyond the Code.

## Optional iCloud sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, Quill stores a copy of your library in your personal iCloud account using Apple's Cloud Documents service. Apple processes that data under your Apple account and Apple's terms. Beyond the Code cannot access your iCloud library. If iCloud is unavailable or sync is disabled, Quill continues to use local storage.

## Permissions

Build 4 requests neither Input Monitoring nor Accessibility. It does not observe or synthesize keystrokes. Quill copies a selected snippet to the system pasteboard; you choose where and when to paste it with Command-V.

## Network access

The Mac App Store build has no general network-client entitlement. Its only optional cloud feature is the Apple-managed iCloud container described above.

## Changes

If Quill's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

## Support

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at [github.com/beyondthecode-bc/Quill/issues](https://github.com/beyondthecode-bc/Quill/issues).
